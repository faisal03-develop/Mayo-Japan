About Us

Company Profile

A Pak-Japan enterprise rooted in ancient salt geology — delivering the world's finest Himalayan Rock Salt products since our founding.

Our Story

Leading Manufacturers & Exporters of Himalayan Rock Salt

We are one of the leading manufacturers and exporters of Himalayan Natural Rock Salt and Rock Salt Products in Pakistan. We have our own Salt Factory at Salt City Quaidabad, District Khushab — at the foot of Warcha Salt Mines, the 2nd largest salt mines in Pakistan after Khewra Salt Mines.

A special rock salt excavated from Warcha Salt Mines is recommended for its unique quality — ideal for premium salt lamps and for producing edible salt of exceptional purity. We are pioneers in making fine-quality Himalayan Rock Salt meeting Japanese food standards, consistently fulfilled for over a decade.

Our efficient, hardworking, and quality-conscious staff work day and night — from excavating salt at the mine to factory processing, shaping lamps, and pressing edible salt — all under strict supervision of food and quality experts.

What We Offer

Full-Spectrum Salt Manufacturing

From raw extraction to finished export-ready product — we handle everything in-house.

🧂
Edible Rock Salt

Fine-quality Himalayan edible salt in various grain sizes (1-2mm, 3-5mm, 5-7mm, 25 Mesh, chunks) meeting Japanese food standards. Dark pink, light pink, and white varieties.

🪨
Natural Salt Lamps

Naturally chiseled lamps in weights from 3 kg to 40 kg, with standard and premium Onyx marble base editions. Unique color and shape variations in every piece.

✨
Crafted Salt Lamps

Machine-precision crafted into 15+ shapes: Guldan, Surahi, Fire Bowl, Tear, Cylinder, Football, Pyramid. Salt tiles (8"×4"×2"), eggs, hearts, soap bars, and massage balls.

🏗️
Salt Tiles & Architecture

Rock salt tiles (8"×4"×2") for spa walls, salt rooms, sauna interiors, and architectural wellness installations.

🛁
Bathing & Wellness

Bathing salt, de-icing salt, massage soap bars, salt eggs, and round massage balls in dark pink and light pink varieties.

🔧
Custom Orders

Fully capable of fulfilling any custom requirement — any shape, size, weight, grain, or specification. We work directly with your sourcing team.
