Mayo Salt Industry

Dedicated to Purity at Every Step

Located at Salt City Quaidabad, District Khushab — directly adjacent to Warcha Salt Mines (the 2nd largest salt mines in Pakistan) — our factory enjoys a unique geographic advantage: raw material arrives fresh from the mine with zero contamination risk.

Our efficient, dedicated staff work around the clock — excavating rock salt, grading it, crafting it into lamp shapes using precision lathe machines and artisanal carving techniques, and pressing edible salt under strict food technologist supervision.

The same facility handles export-grade packaging: heat-sealed, shrink-wrapped, and labeled to meet the destination country's import standards.

Warcha Salt Mines

Our Raw Material Source

🏔️
2nd Largest in Pakistan

Warcha Salt Mines in District Khushab is the second largest salt deposit in Pakistan after the famous Khewra Salt Mines — an abundant, pristine source of Himalayan rock salt.

💎
Premium Quality Salt

The specific geological composition of Warcha salt produces exceptional lamp clarity (warm translucent glow) and high food-grade purity with minimal impurities.

⚗️
84 Natural Minerals

Himalayan crystal rock salt contains exactly the same 84 essential minerals and trace elements found in the human body — unlike chemically refined table salt.

No middlemen. We manufacture everything in-house and supply directly to importers worldwide. Discuss your volume and specification requirements with our team.
