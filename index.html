Who We Are

A Legacy Built on Nature's Finest Salt

We are one of the leading manufacturers and exporters of Himalayan Natural Rock Salt and Rock Salt Products in Pakistan, with our own Salt Factory at Salt City Quaidabad, District Khushab — at the foot of Warcha Salt Mines, the 2nd largest salt mines in Pakistan.

A special rock salt excavated from Warcha Salt Mines is renowned for its unique quality — ideal for premium salt lamps and food-grade edible salt meeting Japanese food standards, which we have fulfilled successfully for over a decade.
